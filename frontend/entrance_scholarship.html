Entrance Award

Entrance Scholarship

Celebrating academic brilliance from the very beginning. Awarded to students who achieved outstanding results in the SSC and HSC public examinations upon their enrolment.

Open for Applications

About This Scholarship

The Entrance Scholarship is awarded to newly enrolled students who have demonstrated remarkable performance in the national public examinations — SSC and HSC. This award honours academic achievement before university begins, establishing a culture of excellence from day one, much as scholarships at institutions like the University of Chittagong and Jahangirnagar University have long done.

Students must apply within the first semester of enrolment to be considered for this award.

Waiver Structure by Result

SSC ResultHSC ResultWaiver
GPA 5.00 (Golden A+)GPA 5.00 (Golden A+)100% Waiver

Eligibility Criteria

  • Newly enrolled student in the current academic year
  • GPA 5.00 in both SSC and HSC
  • Application must be submitted within the first semester
  • Original mark sheets and certificates must be submitted for verification
  • The award is non-renewable and applies to the first semester only

Required Documents

  • Original SSC mark sheet and certificate
  • Original HSC mark sheet and certificate
  • Admission confirmation letter from the institution
  • National ID or birth certificate
  • Completed ELEVATE application form

Application Process

  • 1

    Verify Your Results

    Ensure your SSC and HSC results meet the minimum criteria before applying.

  • 2

    Gather Certificates

    Collect original mark sheets, certificates, and other required documents.

  • 3

    Submit Application

    Complete the ELEVATE form and upload scanned copies of all documents.

  • 4

    Document Verification

    Originals may be requested for physical verification at the scholarship office.

  • 5

    Award Notification

    Decisions communicated within 10 working days of the application deadline.
