Need-Based Scholarship

Ensuring that financial circumstance is never a barrier to quality education. Supporting deserving students from economically disadvantaged backgrounds.

Open for Applications

About This Scholarship

The Need-Based Scholarship reflects ELEVATE's commitment to educational equity — a principle embraced by eminent institutions including BRAC University and North South University, who have long championed inclusive access to higher education.

This scholarship provides meaningful financial relief to students whose family income falls below defined thresholds, empowering them to focus on academic achievement rather than financial anxiety.

Confidentiality Notice All financial information submitted as part of this application is held in strict confidence and used solely for assessment purposes.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Annual family income not exceeding BDT 3,00,000 (supported by income certificate)
  • Currently enrolled as a full-time student in good academic standing
  • Minimum CGPA of 2.75 in the preceding semester
  • No concurrent receipt of another tuition waiver exceeding 25%
  • Evidence of genuine financial hardship as assessed by the scholarship committee
  • Clean disciplinary record at the institution

Award Structure

Required Documents

  • Family income certificate (issued by local government authority)
  • Parent or guardian's tax certificate or affidavit of income
  • Official academic transcript (current semester)
  • National ID of student and guardian
  • Personal statement of financial hardship (400–600 words)
  • Completed ELEVATE application form
