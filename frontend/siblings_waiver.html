Sibling Waiver

Sibling Waiver

A concession in recognition of families who invest in education across multiple members. Applicable when a sibling is currently enrolled at the institution.

✓ You are eligible

About This Scholarship

The Sibling Waiver reflects the institution's appreciation for families who choose to invest in higher education for more than one child. It is a gesture of goodwill and practical support, acknowledging the financial burden of multiple simultaneous enrolments.

Good News This waiver is available to an unlimited number of applicants, provided the sibling enrolment criterion is met. There is no competitive selection — eligibility guarantees the award.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant must have a biological sibling currently enrolled at the institution
  • The sibling must be registered as an active, full-time student
  • Both students must share the same permanent address as listed in official documents
  • Applicant must be in good academic and financial standing with the institution
  • A sibling relationship must be verifiable through official documentation
  • The waiver applies to tuition fees only, not to other institutional charges

Award Structure

Waiver Value10%
Available SlotsUnlimited
DurationPer Semester
RenewableYes (auto)

Required Documents

  • Sibling's current enrolment confirmation (from Registrar's Office)
  • Birth certificates of both applicant and sibling
  • National ID of both students
  • Proof of shared permanent address (utility bill or official letter)
  • Completed ELEVATE application form

Application Process

  • 1

    Verify Sibling Enrolment

    Confirm your sibling is currently registered as an active student at the institution.

  • 2

    Collect Documents

    Obtain the required documents, including the sibling's enrolment confirmation from the Registrar.

  • 3

    Submit Application

    Fill and submit the ELEVATE form before the deadline of 30 July 2026.

  • 4

    Administrative Verification

    The office will verify sibling enrolment directly with the Registrar's records.

  • 5

    Automatic Renewal

    Once approved, the waiver is automatically renewed each semester, provided the sibling remains enrolled.
